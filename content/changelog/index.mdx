# Changelog

Welcome to the Purro changelog! This page tracks all updates, improvements, and new features across our three core components.

## Overview

Purro consists of three main components, each with its own development timeline and release cycle:

- **🏦 DeFi Hub** - Our primary aggregation platform and initial focus
- **🤖 PurroBot** - Advanced market intelligence (evolved from HyperBaby AI)
- **⚡ HyperEVM DEX Aggregator** - Cross-DEX liquidity optimization
